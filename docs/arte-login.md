# Arte do login

Arquivo aplicado: `backend/src/main/resources/static/assets/pigeon-futuristic.png`.

Modo: ferramenta integrada ImageGen, usando como referência a imagem do pombo com óculos fornecida pelo usuário. A primeira arte gerada foi escolhida explicitamente pelo usuário. O arquivo original foi preservado; o efeito neon é uma camada SVG/CSS independente.

## Prompt da arte escolhida

Use case: logo-brand. Create a NEW premium minimalist futuristic mascot emblem for a dark software login page, using the attached image only as a subject reference: a confident serious pigeon seen DIRECTLY FROM THE FRONT wearing aviator sunglasses. HEAD ONLY, a compact cropped head silhouette, no torso, shoulders, wings, feet, background or text. Retain the recognizable broad pigeon forehead, small central pigeon beak and iconic wide sunglasses of the reference. A beautifully designed, balanced, symmetrical professional esports/technology symbol, sophisticated rather than comic, few clean STRAIGHT angular contour segments, excellent negative space, no busy feather details, no circles or round cartoon eyes. TWO COLORS ONLY: light silver-white contour strokes (#D6D8E0) and signal red (#ED1738) for a narrow cybernetic sensor slit in the right side of the sunglasses and one tiny accent if needed. Everything else genuinely TRANSPARENT, including lens interiors and head interior; no black painted background, no filled silver head, no gradients, shading, 3D, textures, realistic feathers, checkerboard or decorative border. Stroke weights substantial enough to read at 220px, precise joins, smooth professional proportion. Centered square asset with modest transparent margins. No letters, watermark, typography or logo text. Final should feel like a polished minimalist angular line logo of the sunglasses pigeon reference, not an eagle, owl or robot helmet.

## Animação

Sensor vermelho em camada separada, ciclo suave de 3,2 segundos com brilho de neon. A preferência `prefers-reduced-motion` mantém iluminação estática.
